# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community, not only for individuals.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable
behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

For private reports, contact the maintainer through the public contact methods
listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile:

https://github.com/Beiyu114514

Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of the
reporter.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will use the following guidance when determining consequences:

1. **Correction** for inappropriate language or minor unprofessional behavior.
2. **Warning** for a single incident or series of minor violations.
3. **Temporary ban** for serious or sustained violations.
4. **Permanent ban** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression.
